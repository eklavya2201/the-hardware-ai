# Core concepts

> Understand how Adam organizes tasks, files, teams, integrations, schedules, and memory.

## Tasks

A task is a conversation with Adam and everything produced during that conversation, including attached and generated files.

Keep related work in one task so Adam can use the earlier conversation and files. Start a new task for unrelated work or when you want to share it with different people.

## Progress, questions, and approvals

After you send a message, Adam shows its progress in the task. It may ask a question or wait for your approval before continuing.

Task status is summarized in the task list:

* **Running** means Adam is still working.
* **Needs input** means a question or approval is waiting for someone who can respond.
* **Completed** means Adam has finished the current request.
* **Scheduled** means the task is set to run later or repeat.

## Files and outputs

Files include anything you upload and anything Adam creates. They can include CAD files, drawings, documents, images, spreadsheets, presentations, code, and data.

The right-hand file panel keeps task outputs close to the conversation. The global **Files** page searches across tasks and lets you preview or export files.

## Teams

Every account starts with a personal team. Shared teams add seats and roles, and share plan usage across members.

Team roles are **Owner**, **Admin**, and **Member**. A task's sharing permissions are separate: a person invited to one task can have **Can edit** or **Can view** access without changing their team role.

## Integrations

Integrations let Adam work with supported products and accounts.

* Connect Onshape or Arena to work with documents and records in those accounts.
* Install the Fusion add-in to work with a design open in Fusion.
* Use the Desktop Connector with supported applications such as SolidWorks, Revit, Blender, SketchUp, and Unreal Engine.

<Note>
  Fusion and other desktop applications need to be open while Adam works with them.
</Note>

## Scheduled work

A scheduled task stores instructions and runs them once or on a recurring schedule. Open **Scheduled** to see upcoming runs and previous results.

## Memory

Memory lets Adam remember useful information across tasks. Open **Settings** → **Memory** to review or remove what Adam remembers.
