## Text-to-CAD answers before you start.

What is CADAM?

CADAM is Adam's text-to-CAD workspace. You describe a model, generate a 3D result, refine the geometry, and export it for downstream use.

What can I generate with CADAM?

CADAM can help with 3D printed parts, early hardware prototypes, product concepts, creative props, game assets, and object studies that need fast iteration.

Do I need CAD experience to use CADAM?

CADAM is useful even without deep CAD experience because it starts from prompts. Engineers can still use it for fast concept exploration before moving into more detailed CAD work.

How is CADAM different from Adam Copilot?

CADAM is the text-to-CAD and AI 3D generation workspace. Adam Copilot is for CAD assistance inside Onshape, Autodesk Fusion, and design workflows that depend on existing CAD context.
