# Changelog

New features, improvements, and fixes across Adam, updated with every release.

August 10, 2026

## Onshape now stays signed in

### The Onshape panel stays signed in
Improvements

Adam inside Onshape now keeps you signed in with a proper browser session, so the panel no longer drops you or asks you to reconnect while you work.

### Large attachments no longer break a turn
Fixes

Oversized images and PDFs are now sized to fit, so a big file no longer fails the message you sent it with.

August 7, 2026

## A new Adam button inside Fusion

### Adam toolbar button in Fusion
New

The Fusion add-in now has its own Adam panel on the SOLID tab, so you can reopen the palette with one click instead of toggling the add-in off and on.

August 4, 2026

## Fusion fixes and live streaming for queued messages

### Less erros in Fusion Extension
Fixes

The first step of most Fusion workflows, reading and opening the active document, no longer fails when a document is already open.

### Agent message streams smoother after queued messages
Fixes

If you queue a message while a run is in progress, the follow-up turn now streams live instead of appearing all at once at the end.

August 3, 2026

## Microphone no longer stays open

### Turning off dictation now turns off the microphone
Fixes

Stopping dictation fully when dictation is turned off, so your browser no longer shows "microphone in use" until you refresh the tab.

### Long conversations cost less
Improvements

Longer conversations now cost less for Pro models

August 1, 2026

## New diction experience, and new password protected chats

### Password-protected share links
New

The share menu's Publish tab now offers Private, Public link, or Password protected, and picking the last one generates a friendly passcode like happy-crane that visitors need to open the link.

### See a task's share state at a glance
New

Share-state indicators now show whether a task is private, publicly linked, or passcode protected without opening the menu.

### Dictation works alongside typing
Improvements

Starting dictation no longer wipes your draft: speech streams in at your caret (replacing any selected text) and you can keep typing while it runs.

### Credit balance is back on the new-task page
Fixes

The credit balance pill has returned to the top right of the new-task page in the web app, matching the Onshape and Fusion panels.

July 31, 2026

## A cleaner approval card in chat

### Approval UI Update
Improvements

Requests for your approval now use the same card style as Adam's questions, with smoother animations and clear Approve and Deny buttons.

July 31, 2026

## Faster STEP Viewer

### STEP files now load faster in the viewer
Improvements

When a STEP file lands in your workspace, whether from an agent, an upload, an in-browser edit, or a transfer from a device, we start preparing its 3D view right away so the viewer opens from cache instead of making you wait.

July 30, 2026

## PDF Rendering Fixes

### No more hot-pink PDFs
Fixes

Gradients in the in-app PDF viewer render properly instead of flashing bright pink, and generated PDFs no longer carry shadows that trip the same rendering path.

July 28, 2026

## Better Q&A UX and faster CAD generations

### Loops stop burning credits
Fixes

Runaway turns that used to spin until the step limit and still bill as successes are now detected and cut short.

### Less thrash on CAD work
Improvements

Adam now spends less time digging around before executing CAD work. Some tool calls that fail in CAD now shows cleanly as errors.

### Same-named attachments no longer collide
Fixes

Uploading two files with the same name in one task now keeps both.

### A cleaner question card in chat
Improvements

The card that the agent uses to ask you a question got a full visual overhaul, including better handling of multi-select, long labels, and open-ended answers.

July 23, 2026

## Improved subscription and billing management

### Manage older subscription plans
Fixes

You can now view and manage legacy plans directly, with the ability to see, cancel, or leave a subscription that previously showed up as Free.

### Faster CAD file viewing
Improvements

Opening a STEP file is faster, especially on repeat views, thanks to a persistent result cache and quicker topology extraction.

July 20, 2026

## Saved drafts and faster STEP viewer

### Snappier STEP viewer
Improvements

Reopening a STEP file is much faster now.

### Composer drafts are saved
New

Typing a message, navigating away, and coming back now keeps your unsent texts.

### Ask Adam about your account
New

Adam can now answer questions about your account, plan, and credit balance on demand.

July 15, 2026

## Better memory and CAD viewer improvements

### Interference detection with highlighting
New

Adam now catches interference and collisions between parts and highlights them in red in the online CAD viewer

### Manage teams from the accounts center
New

The accounts center now has a Team section for shared teams and a link from workspace settings to the full account page.

### Better long-term memory
Improvements

Adam now remembers more of what matters across conversations, with proactive saving, nightly consolidation, and visible memory activity in chat.

July 13, 2026

## A smarter Onshape copilot

### Adam workspace inside Onshape
New

The Onshape panel now carries the full workspace conversation: streaming responses, tool activity, and message history, right next to your document at onshape.adam.new.

### Grok 4.5 in the copilot model pickers
New

Grok 4.5 is available as a model choice in both the Fusion and Onshape copilots.

### Review pass on completed work
Improvements

Adam can run a dedicated adversarial review over finished work to catch mistakes before handing results back to you.

### Clearer activity in the task thread
Improvements

The task thread shows a brand avatar and a live thinking indicator, so it is easier to see when Adam is working.

July 10, 2026

## Files, attachments, and photos

### iPhone photos just work
New

HEIC and HEIF attachments convert to JPEG on attach, with a loading tile while the conversion runs.

July 9, 2026

## Publish your work

### Deploy websites to a permanent public URL
New

Websites built in your workspace can now be deployed to a stable, shareable address that stays up after the task ends.

### Account page and full account deletion
New

A dedicated account page lets you manage sessions and delete your account, with a complete cascade across your data.

### GPT-5.6 Sol
New

GPT-5.6 Sol replaces GPT-5.5 everywhere it was offered.

July 8, 2026

## One Adam account

### Better files on mobile
Improvements

Files open in a fullscreen preview with simplified toolbars built for touch.

### Collapsible sidebar sections
Improvements

Tasks group into collapsible sections, with pinned tasks kept on top and marked with a pin.

### Composer and viewer fixes
Fixes

Backspace no longer deletes attachments when the input is empty, and streaming PDF previews stop looping Safari into endless reflows.

July 7, 2026

## Adam ID and CAD verification

### Interference and fit checks
New

Adam can verify part clearances and interference across Onshape, Fusion, and workspace CAD before you print or machine a design.

### Stripe checkout no longer popup-blocked
Fixes

Upgrading now redirects to checkout in the same tab, so browser popup blockers cannot interrupt it.

July 6, 2026

## Install Adam

### Adam as an installable app
New

The workspace can be installed as an app (PWA) on desktop and mobile, with brand-correct icons.

### @cad references in chat
New

Attach parts of your CAD model to a message as pills, and click a pill to highlight the part in the viewer.

### Richer model viewers
Improvements

The STEP viewer renders authored part colors and gets a restyled inspection panel, and the OpenSCAD viewer gains a customizer panel and smarter camera framing.

July 2, 2026

## Find anything

### Full-text search
New

Search across task titles and message bodies to find past work instantly.

### CAD-aware files grid
New

The files grid gets a CAD file-type filter and real rendered previews of your models.

### Deleting a running task stops its run
Fixes

Deleting a task now cleanly stops any run still in progress.

July 1, 2026

## Models and reliability

### Claude Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.5
New

Both models join the picker, and orchestration moved to GPT-5.5.

### Rebuilt CAD rendering
Improvements

A new offscreen rendering engine with camera presets and part isolation produces faster, cleaner model snapshots.

June 30, 2026

## Blender and SketchUp join the party

### Blender and SketchUp connectors
New

Connect Adam to Blender and SketchUp through the desktop connector and work with them straight from your workspace.

### Feature demos on the copilot page
New

Demo videos on the copilot page now play in a modal on click.

### Tighter access checks on shared files
Security

Shared file reads are authorized against the file's own team, closing a gap in link-based access.

### Markdown export crash
Fixes

The file preview modal no longer crashes when exporting markdown.

June 29, 2026

## Sharing, polished

### A cleaner share sheet
Improvements

Sharing a task now offers separate Share and Publish tabs with consistent controls.
